# My Way Pizza — Content Playbook (shareable brief)
Prepared 2026-09-22 for Regina (content) + Manuel. Source: full Instagram analysis of @mywaypizza305 via browser pull (25 posts, 20 saved posts, 20 DM threads).

## The diagnosis
The account isn't broken — it's dormant. No posts since Aug 3 (~7 weeks). 2,038 followers, 174 posts. When it posts, two formats win: ASMR/crunch reels (best organic: 2,915 views, 89 likes) and event-theater/setup reels (best overall: boosted rain-setup reel, 15.8K views, 519 likes — "the only weather-proof pizza caterer in South Florida" is the winning angle). Photo posts are dead (6–8 likes). Comments are all family; question-CTAs ("what's your topping?") get zero replies — the audience doesn't comment, it DMs (14 of 20 recent threads are sales inquiries, mostly from ads). So: the grid is the portfolio, DMs are the sales floor. Write captions for lurkers, close in DMs.

## Brand rules (non-negotiable)
- NEVER say "wood-fired" (ovens are Ooni electric/gas). Two old captions violate this — edit them.
- Voice: warm neighborhood caterer; posts feel like invitations. Bilingual EN/ES.
- Packages: Traditional $30 / Deluxe $40 / Premium $50 per person; kids 6–12 half price, under 6 free; 50% nonrefundable deposit.

## 4-week posting plan — 3 reels/week
- Tue: CRUNCH/ASMR (undercarriage tap, cheese pull, oven sound — no talking)
- Thu: EVENT THEATER (setup, rain-proof angle, backyard-to-pizzeria transformation)
- Sat: SOCIAL PROOF (reviews, sell-outs, client reactions mid-event)
- Every other week: OFFER (DM keyword "PARTY", date scarcity — "October has 3 dates left")
- Kill: photo-only posts, generic question captions, personal collab content on the business grid.

## Content FAQ (real customer questions from DMs — turn each into a reel or FAQ highlight)

2. "What are the prices?" → package tiers, fast.
3. "Which package fits my event?" → guide, don't menu-dump. (Kids count matters.)
4. "Will it fit?" → 10×10 ft footprint, countertop ovens.
5. "How does booking work?" → 50% nonrefundable deposit, arrive ~1h before service.
6. Kids pricing: under 6 free, 6–12 half — put it in the FAQ highlight.
