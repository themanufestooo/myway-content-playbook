Content FAQ — real customer questions

  • "Do you offer delivery?" → "No delivery. We bring the pizzeria to you."

  • "Which package fits my event?" → guide them, don't menu-dump. (Kids count matters.)
  • "Will it fit?" → 10×10 ft footprint, countertop ovens.
  • "How does booking work?" → 50% nonrefundable deposit, crew arrives ~1h before service.
  • Kids pricing (under 6 free, 6–12 half) → pin it in the FAQ highlight.
